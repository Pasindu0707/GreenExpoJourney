The monsoons

Two seasons, two coasts

May to September

South-west monsoon

Rain in the south and west. The east and north are at their best.

November to February

North-east monsoon

Rain in the north and east. The south, west and hills are clear.

October

In between

Showers anywhere. Quieter, and lower prices.
