12 days

The Classic Loop

Coconut country, Sigiriya, Kandy, tea country, Yala and the south coast. The best first visit.

Best December to April

Day by day
  1. Day 1Arrive at Katunayake. Evening on Negombo lagoon.
  2. Day 2Our coconut estate in Dankotuwa and lunch with us, then on to Sigiriya.
  3. Day 3Climb Sigiriya at first light. Dambulla cave temples in the afternoon.
  4. Day 4A family spice garden in Matale, then Kandy for the evening puja.
  5. Day 5Kandy: the botanical gardens at Peradeniya and the lake.
  6. Day 6Up into tea country to an estate bungalow near Nuwara Eliya.
  7. Day 7Pluck with the morning shift and taste at the factory.
  8. Day 8The hill-country train to Ella. Walk to the Nine Arch Bridge.
  9. Day 9Sunrise on Little Adam’s Peak, then down to Yala.
  10. Day 10Dawn safari in Yala, then along the coast to Mirissa.
  11. Day 11Whale watching (November to April) or a slow beach day.
  12. Day 12Galle Fort in the morning, then the expressway to the airport.

4 days

Lagoons and Dolphins

The north-west coast most visitors skip: Negombo, our coconut estates, Chilaw and Kalpitiya.

Dolphins November to April, kitesurfing May to October

Day by day
  1. Day 1Lellama fish market at dawn, a boat on Negombo lagoon and the Hamilton Canal.
  2. Day 2Our estate and lunch in Dankotuwa, then Munneswaram temple in Chilaw.
  3. Day 3Birds at Anawilundawa early, the salt pans at Puttalam, then on to Kalpitiya.
  4. Day 4A dawn dolphin trip or a kitesurfing lesson, depending on the season. About 3 hours back to the airport.

6 days

Jaffna and the East

The Tamil north, Delft Island and the quiet beaches of Trincomalee.

Best February to September

Day by day
  1. Day 1Train from Colombo to Jaffna, about 7 hours through the dry zone.
  2. Day 2Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil, the fort, the library and crab curry for dinner.
  3. Day 3Ferry to Delft Island: wild ponies, coral walls and a Dutch-era ruin or two.
  4. Day 4Casuarina Beach in the morning, then about 4 hours to Trincomalee.
  5. Day 5Snorkel at Pigeon Island, then Koneswaram temple at sunset.
  6. Day 6Whale watching (about March to August), then back towards the airport.

5 days

Tea Country by Train

Kandy to Ella on the hill line, with two nights on a working tea estate.

Best January to April

Day by day
  1. Day 1Kandy: the Temple of the Tooth and the lake.
  2. Day 2Train from Kandy up to Nanu Oya, then to your estate bungalow.
  3. Day 3Plucking with the morning shift and a factory tour with tasting.
  4. Day 4Train to Ella. Walk the line to the Nine Arch Bridge.
  5. Day 5Sunrise on Little Adam’s Peak, then onward wherever you’re heading.

6 days

Leopards and the South

Yala safaris, whale watching from Mirissa and two slow days in Galle Fort.

Best December to April

Day by day
  1. Day 1Drive to Yala. Afternoon safari.
  2. Day 2A full day in the park: dawn and dusk drives, siesta in between.
  3. Day 3Along the coast to Mirissa.
  4. Day 4Whale watching at dawn (November to April).
  5. Day 5Stilt fishermen at Koggala, then into Galle Fort.
  6. Day 6Galle Fort’s ramparts and lanes, then about 2.5 hours to the airport.

1 day

A Day with Us

Negombo’s fish market and lagoon, then lunch at our place in Dankotuwa. Good for a first or last day.

All year

Day by day
  1. Early: Lellama fish market as the boats come in.
  2. Mid-morning: a boat through the lagoon’s mangroves.
  3. Lunch at our coconut estate in Dankotuwa and a look at how we process.
  4. Back to your hotel or the airport by late afternoon.
