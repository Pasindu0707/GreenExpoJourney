FAQ

Seasons, answered

When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

It depends on the coast. December to April is best for the west and south coasts and the hill country. April to September is best for the east coast and the north. Somewhere on the island is usually dry in every month.

When is whale watching in Sri Lanka?

Off Mirissa on the south coast, roughly November to April. Off Trincomalee on the east coast, roughly March to August.

When can you climb Sri Pada (Adam’s Peak)?

The pilgrim season runs from about December to May, when the path is lit at night and the weather is clearest.

Is Yala National Park open all year?

No. Yala usually closes in September and the first half of October.
