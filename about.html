About us

We’re exporters first. Travel is how we share the places and people we work with.

From exporting to hosting

Green Expo was started in 2012 by two partners in Dankotuwa, each with more than ten years in the food export trade. We export coconut products, spices, tea and traditional Sri Lankan foods.

Over the years, buyers kept asking to see where their cinnamon and tea came from. Green Expo Journeys is our answer: private trips around the island, led by the same team, with visits to the estates and gardens we know.

Our office is in Dankotuwa, about 40 minutes from the airport. If you have a spare afternoon, come for lunch.

What to expect

Tell us how you like to travel and we’ll plan around it.

  • Your own car and driver-guide

    Private trips only. You set the pace and the stops.

  • Farm and estate visits

    Coconut, spice and tea, through people we buy from.

  • Stays we’ve checked

    From estate bungalows to beach guesthouses, at the budget you choose.

  • One contact throughout

    Message us on WhatsApp any time during your trip.
