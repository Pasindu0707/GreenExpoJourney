Our story

From Dankotuwa, since 2012

Two partners started Green Expo to export coconut, spice and tea. Buyers kept asking to see where it all came from, so now we show them, on private tours across the island.

FAQ

Questions

Are your Sri Lanka tours private?

Yes. Every trip is private, with your own car and driver-guide, and you set the pace and the stops.

Can you pick us up from the airport?

Yes. We meet you at arrivals at Bandaranaike International Airport in Katunayake.

Can we visit a tea plantation or a spice garden?

Yes. Our trips can include tea estates near Nuwara Eliya, spice gardens in Matale and our own coconut estate in Dankotuwa.

How do I book a tour?

Send an enquiry from the Plan a trip page, email info@greenexpolk.com or WhatsApp +94 71 148 6677. We reply within a day with a plan and a price. There is no booking fee.
